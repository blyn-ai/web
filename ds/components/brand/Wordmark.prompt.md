Use Wordmark for the company name in any lockup; pair it with LogoMark to the left.

```jsx
<a href="/" style={{ display: "inline-flex", alignItems: "center", gap: 12 }}>
  <LogoMark size="md" />
  <Wordmark tone="ink" />
</a>
```

- The pipes in `name` mark the gold segment: `"Blyn|AI| Capital"`. Never bold or italicise other letters.
- `size="lg"` is the social-card size (27px name / 13px descriptor).
- The legal name is written `MB „BlynAI“` in body copy — the wordmark is the trade name only.
