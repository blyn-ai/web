Use Section for every band of a page so gutters and separators stay identical down the scroll.

```jsx
<Section eyebrow="Tyrimų kryptys">
  <NumberedList items={items} />
</Section>
```

- Alternate `paper` and `raised` down the page; never more than two background values per document.
- `wash` is for the hero only: gold field top-left, violet field bottom-right.
