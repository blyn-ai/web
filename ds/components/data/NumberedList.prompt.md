Use NumberedList for an ordered set of commitments or research directions — the brand's preferred alternative to a card grid.

```jsx
<NumberedList items={[{ title: "Paaiškinamas sprendimas", body: "Kiekvienas orderis įrašomas…" }]} />
```

- Four items is the canonical length; the colour ramp is built for four.
- Rows are separated by hairlines only. Do not box them.
