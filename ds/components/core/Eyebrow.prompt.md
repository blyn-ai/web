Use Eyebrow above a section heading to name the section in the brand's registry voice.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Tyrimų kryptys</Eyebrow>
<h2>Trys klausimai, į kuriuos ieškome atsakymo</h2>
```

- Two to four words. Often a middle dot instead of a comma: "Mažoji bendrija · Lietuva".
- Violet on paper, gold on ink. Never both on one surface.
