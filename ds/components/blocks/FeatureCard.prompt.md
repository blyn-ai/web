Use FeatureCard in a 3- or 4-up grid on dark surfaces, where NumberedList would be too quiet.

```jsx
<FeatureCard index="01" title="Ar sprendimą galima paaiškinti?">Kiekvienam pirkimui…</FeatureCard>
```

- One paragraph, 3-4 lines. If it needs two paragraphs it belongs in a NumberedList row.
- `tone="glass"` for cards over a wash or curve; `tone="ink"` over flat ink.
