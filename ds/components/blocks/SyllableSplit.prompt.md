Use SyllableSplit once per page, to explain the name. It is the brand's signature block.

```jsx
<SyllableSplit
  left={{ word: "BLYN", expansion: "Blockchain Ledger Yield Numerics", note: "Kas matuojama…" }}
  right={{ word: "AI", expansion: "Algorithmic Inference", note: "Kas iš to daroma…" }}
  note="Lietuviškai tas pats žodis skaitomas kaip „blynai“…"
/>
```

- Gold is always left, violet always right. The 2px ink rule above the split is required.
- The footnote is where the brand admits the pun; keep it, and keep it dry.
